Executive Summary

Both platforms are built for tint shops. One was built by a tinter.

Tint Wiz is a mature CRM that has served the tint industry since 2018. It handles scheduling, invoicing, inventory, and staff management well. It is the safe, known choice — and for many shops, it is the right one.

Smiley's Hub is a newer platform with a different philosophy. We built it because the biggest problems we faced as a working tint shop — damage disputes, unprotected high-value protective film jobs, unproven customers ghosting on proposals, and customers who pay once and never return — were not being solved by any existing CRM. So we built them into ours.

Bottom line: Smiley's Hub matches Tint Wiz on nearly every operational feature. On customer-facing experience, inspection defense, and financial depth, it goes significantly further. And on price, we are comparable or less.

The Moat

Where Smiley's Hub goes further

These are not small increments. Each of these is a category of capability Tint Wiz does not offer today — and each one has a direct revenue or risk impact on a working shop.

1. Signed inspection reports — your legal protection

The #1 source of disputes in tinting is pre-existing damage. A customer swears the windshield was clean when they dropped the car off. Without photographic evidence and a signature, you lose that argument — every time. Smiley's Hub walks you through a 6-step inspection protocol with timestamped photos, damage mapping, a VLT baseline, and a customer signature on file before film ever touches glass. Not a single CRM on the market offers this.

2. AI damage detection — turn a 10-minute inspection into 30 seconds

Upload a photo. AI scans it, identifies dents, chips, scratches, and glass damage with severity scoring and estimated repair cost. This is the future of vehicle inspection — and we are the only tint shop platform offering it today.

3. 360° viewer + live walkthrough — close more high-ticket jobs

Commercial, residential, and marine proposals often close remotely. Smiley's Hub lets you share a live 360° view of the vehicle or space with a customer on their own phone, in real time. Tint Wiz stops at a PDF. We close the deal with an experience.

4. Pre-inspection gate — protect every protection job

Ceramic coating, PPF, wraps, and paint correction are the highest-margin, highest-risk services you offer. If something goes wrong on a $2,500 wrap and there's no inspection on file, you eat the loss. Smiley's Hub blocks these bookings until a signed inspection exists. No competitor offers this gate.

5. Coating Club — turn one-time customers into $1,020/year

Most tint shops lose their ceramic coating customers after the first wash. Smiley's Hub automatically enrolls every ceramic customer into a monthly membership program with reminder sequences, retention tracking, at-risk flagging, and auto-booking. Fourteen members at $85/month is $1,190 in recurring revenue Tint Wiz cannot help you capture.

6. Full financial depth — run your business from one screen

Tint Wiz shows you analytics. Smiley's Hub shows you a full P&L with revenue broken down by service line, gross margin per class, a 13-week rolling cash flow forecast, A/R aging buckets, and a complete chart of accounts. It's QuickBooks reporting built specifically for tint shops.

7. Room-by-room proposals with film education

When Smiley sells a $6,200 residential job, the customer needs to understand what they're buying. Our proposals show every room, the specific film selected, its description and benefits, and the price. No square footage confusion. No rate math. Just clarity — which closes more deals.
